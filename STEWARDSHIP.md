# Our stewardship promise

Localization OS is open core: the core in this repository is free under the Apache 2.0 license, and Patois Labs
sells hosting, support, services and some paid add-ons around it. These promises tell you what you can rely on.

1. **Nothing that is free ever becomes paid.** A feature released in the open source core stays in the open source
   core.
2. **Some things are always free:** single sign-on (SSO), SCIM provisioning, the audit chain, accessibility, and
   security fixes. New work in these areas goes into the free core.
3. **Paid add-ons may join the core later.** Moving a feature from paid to free is allowed; moving free to paid is
   not.
4. **No artificial limits in the core.** We do not cap users, projects, words or languages in the open source code
   to push you toward paying.
5. **The core works on its own.** It never depends on a paid add-on to run, and your data stays yours: you can export
   everything at any time.

Changes to this page are recorded in its git history, and they only ever add to these commitments.
