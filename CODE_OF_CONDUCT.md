# Code of conduct

Localization OS follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be welcoming, respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and
publishing other people's private information are not acceptable anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat and events.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **support@patoislabs.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers,
who may take any action they consider appropriate, from a warning to a permanent ban, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
